# The prompts

Each prompt works here or pasted into any Claude chat. Replace the parts in [brackets].

## 1. Finding reels worth remaking

Look for **outliers**: reels that did far better than the account usually does. They prove the idea, not the creator's fame.

1. Search your niche on Instagram or TikTok (or open the accounts you'd like to be like).
2. On each account, note the views on its last 10 to 20 reels and find its usual number (the middle one).
3. A reel with **at least 5 times** that number is an outlier. Save it.
4. Prefer outliers that: are under 60 seconds, have a hook you can say in under 8 words, and a format you can make (talking, animated, text over B-roll).
5. Keep 10 to 30 in a list with the link, the views, the account's usual views, and the hook.

Prompt to rank a list:

> Here are reels from my niche, each with views and the account's usual views: [list]. Rank them by how far they beat their account's usual reel. For the top 5, say in one line what idea or hook made them spread, and whether someone with [my topic / my account] could remake the structure honestly.

## 2. Breaking a reel down

> Here's the transcript of a reel that got [views] views, about [topic]: [transcript]. [What's on screen, if known.]
>
> Break it down so I can reuse the structure:
> 1. The hook: the exact words, the pattern it uses (contradiction, promise, open loop, list, story, "nobody tells you"), and why it stops the scroll.
> 2. Each beat after it, line by line: the words, the job it does (setup, proof, turn, payoff, call to action), and roughly how long it lasts.
> 3. The turn: where it flips or surprises.
> 4. The call to action: what it asks people to do and what it offers.
> 5. Pacing: total words, length, words per second, number of beats.
> 6. Why it works, in three plain sentences.

## 3. Writing your version

> Using this breakdown: [breakdown], write a new reel script about [my topic / offer] for [who it's for].
>
> Keep the same skeleton: the same hook pattern, the same number of beats in the same order, about the same length ([N] words). Use my topic and my own words; don't reuse any of the original lines. Only make claims that are true for me: [what I can honestly claim]. End with: [call to action, e.g. "Comment GUIDE and I'll send it to you"].
>
> Plain spoken English, like a friend explaining it. No jargon, no "in 30 seconds" style time claims. Under 8 words for the hook. Plain text only, just the words to be read. Then give me two other hooks in the same pattern.

## 4. Hooks only

> Here's my reel script: [script]. Write 10 hooks for it, each under 8 words, in different patterns: 3 contradictions ("X isn't Y. It's Z."), 3 promises ("Here's how..."), 2 open loops ("Nobody tells you this about..."), 2 surprising numbers. Mark the 2 you'd test first and say why in one line each.

## 5. Making it

Paste the finished script here and say "make a reel with this script". The make-reel skill takes it from there: voice, pictures, animation, captions, checks, render and sound.
