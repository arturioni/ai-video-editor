It's not the food. It's the plane. At cruising height, the cabin air is drier than most deserts. Your nose dries out, and most flavor is really smell. The low pressure weakens your taste buds too. Salt and sugar taste about a third weaker. So airlines add extra salt and spice to make up for it. But here's the weird part. The engine noise dulls sweet, but it boosts savory. That's one reason people crave tomato juice on planes. Save this for your next flight.
