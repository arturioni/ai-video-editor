# Worked example: why airplane food tastes so bland

The reel the kit makes from the one line "make a reel about why airplane food tastes so bland" (about 27 seconds), in the neutral Clean starter look. `airplane-food.mp4` is a lighter copy to watch; your own renders come out sharper.

`scenes.py` names no colours or fonts: rebuild it after setting up your style and it comes out in your look.

- `script.txt`: 85 words, a hook under 8 words, one idea per sentence, a turn ("But here's the weird part"), a call to action.
- `scenes.py`: ten scenes. Three photos (desert, wing, tomato juice), six drawn scenes (big words, a falling number, a drawn nose, salt shaker, ear and sound waves, a plane), one takeover on the turn, three transitions where the topic shifts.
- `assets/f/`: the three photos after `pictures use` (all CC0; see `credits.json`).

To rebuild it yourself: copy this folder to `work/airplane-food/`, then run `voice`, `build`, `check`, `render`, `sound` and `finish` on it.

The claims: cabin air at cruising height is often drier than desert air (around 10 to 20% humidity); dry air and low cabin pressure dull salt and sweet by about 30% (a Lufthansa / Fraunhofer study); loud noise dulls sweet and boosts savory (umami) (Cornell, 2015).
